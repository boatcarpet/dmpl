How it works

  1. Pick your team at the top (this device remembers it).
  2. Tap the dates and times your team can play. Add any other time that works with “Other time.”
  3. When both teams have a time in common, it shows on the match and everyone on both teams gets an email. The home team taps Book and reserves a court.
  4. Once it's booked, both teams get a confirmation email, then a reminder the day before. Need to move a match? Tap Unbook and pick again.
  5. Still enter scores on Tenniscores as usual.
